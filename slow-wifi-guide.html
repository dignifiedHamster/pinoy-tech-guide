What to Check When Wi-Fi Is Slow

Slow Wi-Fi can come from your device, router, wireless signal, or internet connection. Before contacting your internet provider, try this simple checklist.

1. Check if the problem affects other devices

Try another phone or computer on the same Wi-Fi network. If only one device is slow, the problem may be related to that device rather than the whole connection.

2. Restart your router

Turn off your router, wait briefly, and turn it back on. Give it a few minutes to reconnect before testing the connection again.

3. Move closer to the router

Walls, floors, furniture, and other obstacles can weaken a Wi-Fi signal. Test your connection closer to the router to see whether the speed improves.

4. Check what is using your connection

Large downloads, video streaming, cloud backups, game updates, and many connected devices can use a lot of bandwidth. Pause unnecessary downloads and test again.

5. Try the other Wi-Fi band

Many modern routers provide 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz networks. A 5 GHz connection can often provide higher speeds at shorter distances, while 2.4 GHz can travel farther and may work better through obstacles.

6. Forget and reconnect to the Wi-Fi

On your phone or computer, forget the Wi-Fi network and connect again using the correct password. This can help when the device has a connection configuration problem.

7. Run a speed test

If possible, compare your connection speed at different times of day and in different locations. Remember that speed-test results can vary depending on the test server and network conditions.

8. Check for router or internet-provider problems

If several devices are slow even when they are close to the router, the issue may be with the router, modem, or your internet service. Check your provider's official service-status information if available.

Quick tip: Don't immediately assume that buying a new router will fix slow internet. First identify whether the problem is Wi-Fi signal, a particular device, or the internet connection itself.

Final thoughts

Start with the easiest checks: test another device, restart the router, move closer to it, and pause heavy downloads. If multiple devices remain slow after these checks, contacting your internet provider may be the next step.
